Robert Potter

IT Systems Architect

I am Robert Potter, an IT Systems Architect based in the Milwaukee, Wisconsin area.

My foundation in information technology began early, when I was architecting and hosting multiplayer server environments at age ten. Today, my career is anchored in the structural logic of enterprise IT. I specialize in aligning business strategy with technical execution, driving standardization across large regional footprints.

My core engineering work includes enterprise platform readiness, IoT network segmentation, edge computing infrastructure, and the live-environment orchestration of high-availability regional networks.

In my independent research, I explore local-first edge computing, knowledge graphs, and agentic state management. I am the author of the Context Inversion Protocol (CIP) and founder of the Sovereign OS Project. CIP is a proposed edge-first profile for persistent human context. Sovereign OS Control Plane 1.6.0 is a published local custody prototype, not a completed CIP runtime.

Beyond systems engineering, I maintain a fine art portrait practice at robertpotter.art, dedicated to archival pigment portraiture. Both my technical architecture and my physical artwork share a unified philosophy: a rejection of speed and extraction in favor of slowness, sovereignty, and permanence.
